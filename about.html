About Bridgepoint

Twenty-two years of showing up before the cameras do.

Mission

Deliver health, education and emergency relief to communities facing crisis, and account for every dollar in the open.

Vision

A world where a community's recovery is never slowed by a funder's uncertainty about where the money goes.

Core Values

Local leadership, radical transparency, dignity in delivery, and evidence over anecdote.
