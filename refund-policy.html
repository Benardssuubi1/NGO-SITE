Refund Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Requesting a refund

If you made a donation in error — a duplicate charge, an incorrect amount, or a mistaken recurring signup — contact us within 30 days at hello@bridgepointrelief.org with your transaction reference and we will process a refund to your original payment method.

Processing time

Approved refunds are issued within 5–10 business days. The time it takes to appear on your statement depends on your card issuer or bank.

Recurring gifts

You may cancel a monthly recurring gift at any time; cancellation stops future charges but does not automatically refund past ones. Contact us if a specific past charge needs review.

Non-refundable circumstances

Gifts that have already been disbursed to field programs more than 30 days prior generally cannot be refunded, since funds have been committed to program delivery. We'll always explain the specific status of your gift when you reach out.

Questions about this policy? Contact us.
